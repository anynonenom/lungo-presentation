# Lungo Presentation — Design Direction

## Ground-truth reference

The supplied Lungo brand board is the source of truth. The presentation must feel like a natural extension of that world: **cream paper, warm sand, mocha, olive, sage, charcoal, natural light, tactile surfaces, raw brush marks, thin line icons, and editorial coffee photography**. It should not look like a generic agency deck or a technology pitch.

## Chosen approach: The Quiet Manifesto

### Design movement

Contemporary **slow-living editorial design**, shaped by independent coffee journals, tactile brand books, and restrained Swiss typography softened by handmade Moroccan warmth.

### Core principles

1. **Quiet confidence:** every slide carries one clear idea, with short language and generous air.
2. **Taste made visible:** product detail, ritual, origin, and texture prove quality without loud claims.
3. **Editorial rhythm:** alternate immersive photography, large manifesto lines, and clear strategic frameworks.
4. **Human imperfection:** brush marks, paper grain, hand-drawn underlines, and off-axis compositions keep the system warm.

### Color philosophy

Cream is the breathing space and makes the deck feel considered rather than corporate. Charcoal carries authority without harsh black. Olive is the ownable signal of taste, nature, and Lungo's calm confidence. Sand and mocha add warmth and connect the digital deck to coffee, wood, pastry, and sunlit interiors. Sage is used sparingly for softer secondary moments.

### Layout paradigm

The deck uses **asymmetric editorial spreads** rather than centered card grids. Text sits on deliberate margins, images break the frame, and slide numbers behave like magazine folios. Strategic diagrams stay simple and linear so the owner understands each idea in seconds.

### Signature elements

- A raw olive brushstroke used as underline, divider, or transition marker.
- A small **L.** monogram placed like an editorial signature.
- Fine black rules, circular stamps, and small uppercase labels inspired by the supplied identity board.

### Interaction philosophy

The presentation should feel calm and effortless. Vertical scrolling, keyboard arrows, click navigation, and a discreet progress rail make it easy to present live. Controls stay quiet until needed. No noisy effects or gimmicks.

### Animation

Slides reveal with restrained opacity and 16–24px vertical movement. Image masks open slowly, while text follows in a short stagger. Transitions use a confident ease-out. Motion never competes with the speaker and is disabled for reduced-motion users.

### Typography system

**Manrope** is used as the practical substitute for PP Neue Montreal: large, clean, modern headlines with tight tracking and strong weight contrast. **Newsreader Italic** appears only for human, reflective lines. Small labels use uppercase Manrope with generous letter spacing. Arabic and darija use **Noto Sans Arabic** for legibility and visual harmony.

### Brand essence

**Lungo is Agadir's circle of taste: for people who choose substance over noise, built one deliberate cup and one good moment at a time.** Personality: warm, selective, thoughtful.

### Brand voice

Headlines are short and certain. CTAs invite rather than push. Microcopy feels like a friend with taste: clear, calm, and honest. English gives the editorial edge; darija gives the soul.

Example lines:

> We are not here to make Lungo louder. We are here to make it clear.

> Slow down. The coffee isn't going anywhere. و عوم بحرك.

### Wordmark & logo

Use the existing long, low Lungo wordmark logic and pair it with a bold **L.** symbol. The symbol is a simple charcoal right-angle stroke and dot, occasionally enclosed in an olive circular stamp. It must read clearly in the presentation header and favicon.

### Signature brand color

**Lungo Olive — `#686F4A`**. It is earthy, cultured, calm, and distinct enough to carry the full strategic idea without becoming decorative.

## Presentation goal

The deck must create two feelings at once: **excitement about what Lungo can become** and **clarity about exactly how EIDEN will make it happen**. Every slide should pass a simple test: can the idea be understood in ten seconds, without complicated wording?

## Narrative arc

1. Open with the ambition.
2. Name the hidden truth: Lungo is a taste club.
3. Explain what Lungo truly sells: time, taste, and belonging.
4. Show the strategic position.
5. Introduce the people we want to attract.
6. Build the content world through Ritual, Source, and Philosophy.
7. Make the tone of voice tangible.
8. Turn tasting events into the signature format.
9. Explain the content rhythm and channel system.
10. Clarify EIDEN's role and the working process.
11. Show the first 90 days and what success looks like.
12. Close with the shared ambition and next step.

## Quality bar

The result should feel premium enough for both EIDEN and Lungo, but never complicated. It should look designed, not decorated; persuasive, not salesy; and specific to Lungo, not reusable for another coffee shop.

## Style Decisions

The visual review sharpened three non-negotiable rules for the delivered system. **Editorial photography is a recurring proof layer**, using warm coffee, hands, tables, interiors, product detail, and Agadir material texture so strategy never becomes typography-only. **The Lungo signature always combines a deliberate long-low wordmark with the bold L. monogram**, making the identity authored rather than typed. **Every major chapter carries one tactile human mark**—an olive stroke, cup-ring trace, stamped note, paper grain, or off-axis annotation—kept restrained enough to preserve quiet confidence.
